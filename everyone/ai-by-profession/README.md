## 🔑 KEY TRENDS ACROSS VERTICALS (2025–2026)

1. **Agentic AI is now standard** — Multi-step autonomous workflows (not just chat) dominate legal, sales, customer service, and finance.
2. **Per-outcome pricing** — Intercom's $0.99/resolved conversation model is spreading; pay-per-result replaces per-seat for AI agents.
3. **Enterprise pricing opacity** — Most vertical AI tools don't publish pricing (Harvey, Sierra, Gong). "Book a demo" remains the norm.
4. **Consolidation** — Thomson Reuters owns CoCounsel + Westlaw AI; S&P Global owns Kensho; Luma bundles dozens of models under one subscription.
5. **Accuracy demands** — Stanford found legal AI hallucinated 17–33% of the time; healthcare requires 99.9%+. Vertical tools differentiate on grounding and verification.
6. **Valuations exploding** — Sierra ($15.8B), Harvey ($11B), Clay ($5B), Suno ($2.45B) show vertical AI commanding premium multiples.
