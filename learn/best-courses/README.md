### 📄 Papers Worth Reading (Foundational & Influential)

#### Foundational Architecture

| Paper | Year | Why It Matters |
|-------|------|---------------|
| **Attention Is All You Need** (Vaswani et al.) | 2017 | Introduced the Transformer architecture — the foundation of all modern LLMs | https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762 |
| **BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers** (Devlin et al.) | 2018 | Bidirectional pre-training; started the "pre-train then fine-tune" era | https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805 |
| **Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3)** (Brown et al.) | 2020 | Demonstrated in-context learning and scaling laws | https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165 |
| **Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models** (Kaplan et al.) | 2020 | Power-law relationship between model size, data, and performance | https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361 |
| **Training Compute-Optimal LLMs (Chinchilla)** (Hoffmann et al.) | 2022 | Showed most LLMs were undertrained; reshaped training budgets | https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556 |

#### Reasoning & Prompting

| Paper | Year | Why It Matters |
|-------|------|---------------|
| **Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning** (Wei et al.) | 2022 | Breakthrough showing LLMs reason better step-by-step | https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903 |
| **Tree of Thoughts** (Yao et al.) | 2023 | Deliberate problem-solving via branching reasoning | https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601 |
| **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting** (Yao et al.) | 2022 | Foundation for tool-using agents; reason + act interleaved | https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629 |
| **Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought** (Wang et al.) | 2022 | Sample multiple reasoning paths, take majority vote | https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171 |
| **Constitutional AI** (Bai et al.) | 2022 | AI self-improvement through constitutional principles (Anthropic) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073 |

#### Alignment & RLHF

| Paper | Year | Why It Matters |
|-------|------|---------------|
| **Training Language Models to Follow Instructions (InstructGPT)** (Ouyang et al.) | 2022 | RLHF applied to LLMs; foundation of ChatGPT | https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155 |
| **Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)** (Rafailov et al.) | 2023 | Simplified alternative to RLHF, widely adopted | https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290 |
| **RLHF: Training Helpful and Harmless Assistants** (Bai et al.) | 2022 | Anthropic's foundational RLHF paper | https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.05862 |

#### RAG & Tool Use
